# StudyOrbital S8.1 — structure and layout rules

## Visual system retained
- Navy/charcoal surfaces with restrained teal accents.
- Compact typography, modest corner radii, consistent borders, no new gradients or loud effects.
- Existing tracker cards, data tables, forms, charts, and modal styles are preserved.

## Navigation hierarchy
- Desktop: Overview, then Study (Chapter Plan, Question Bank, Daily Log), Performance (My Tests, Analysis), and Records (Leave Records, Data & Backup, Personal Storage).
- Mobile: Home, Plan, Daily Log, My Tests, and a grouped More menu.
- The overview no longer repeats every destination as a second card catalogue or repeats the progress/consistency/hour metrics in a separate rotating insight card. The original summary dashboard remains the single visible source for those figures. A single prominent action starts a Daily Log. Global search remains one compact utility.
- To-do is docked at the right of the Study Workspace greeting. Its dimensions and icon/label treatment are not enlarged or redesigned.

## Credit panel
The creator credit appears once, in a full-width rectangular panel at the end of the page flow. Architect, author tag, age, and “Developed by Passion” are separated into clear elements with restrained symbols. It stays at the bottom for short pages and follows content on long pages.

## Data integrity
S8.1 changes shell/navigation placement only. It retains tracker IDs, tracker storage schema, existing calculations, localStorage key, and Personal Storage IndexedDB key. Cloud login remains inactive until an owner-controlled Supabase project is configured and tested.
